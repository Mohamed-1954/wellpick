# BENCHMARK.md (29 Sep 2026)

Section-by-section benchmark of The Client Stack against seven live SaaS sites. Captured with Playwright (Edge channel) by `scripts/bench/capture.mjs`. Each page loaded at 1440×900 and 390×844 (mobile UA), scrolled in 70%-viewport steps with a running-animation log, and returned to the top. Then the script took a full-page screenshot (capped at 16,000px) and read computed styles. `scripts/bench/summarize.mjs` prints the measurements. `scripts/bench/slice.mjs` cuts the full pages into readable segments (`__sNN.png`).

- Ours (before): `.impeccable/live/bench-2026-09-29/before/` (static build on `astro preview :4322`, env ribbon hidden by injected CSS)
- Benchmarks: `.impeccable/live/bench-2026-09-29/bench/`
- Measurement dumps: `bench-1440.txt`, `bench-390.txt`, `ours-1440.txt`, `ours-390.txt` in the same folder

Screenshot names are `<id>__<width>__{hero,full,sNN}.png`. Paths below are relative to `.impeccable/live/bench-2026-09-29/`. That folder is excluded from git through `.git/info/exclude`.

**Access notes.** All seven sites loaded under automation. ClickUp's desktop pages timed out on the first run and loaded on a retry. `unicorn.studio/pricing` redirects to a login screen, so Unicorn Studio has no pricing counterpart. Mintlify's product diagrams render to `<canvas>` after scroll and are blank in the stills. Their structure is read from the DOM and the motion log.

**How to read "measured".** Values are computed styles, in px: size/line-height, weight, letter-spacing. "Container" is the most frequent width of centered blocks.

## Motion observed on the benchmarks

| Site | Moments that animate (from the scroll log) |
| --- | --- |
| ClickUp | Hero load (12 running animations on arrival), logo marquee; blog cards fade and rise as they enter; TOC highlight follows scroll |
| Airtable | Almost none: an opacity progress indicator on the chaptered section; static stacked panels |
| Customer.io | Logo marquee; word-by-word headline reveal (WAAPI spans) |
| Mintlify | Stats marquee, carousel dimming, SVG path translate and opacity in the product diagram as it enters |
| Amplemarket | Webflow IX nav state, product-screen transforms on scroll, dot-nav on the long compare article |
| Huly | Video-driven hero, gradient text sweep late on the page |
| Unicorn Studio | WebGL canvases, one marquee |

Takeaway for us: the meaningful moments are a hero reveal and a diagram that draws as it enters (Mintlify). We already have exactly those (3D hero, draw-once connectors). No new motion is added. The new StackMap and home connectors reuse the existing `data-draw` hook, and reduced motion shows them fully drawn.
