## Identity (v12)
Name: Wellpick ("well picked"). Mark (`Logo.astro`): one round stroke drawn as a W whose last stroke rises past the others into a tick, running through the three chapter colors in workflow order (lifted variants `--logo-1..3` on dark surfaces); `tone="mono"` for single-color use. Wordmark: Archivo 800, `wdth` 104, −0.04em. Favicon: the mark on a paper tile (`public/favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico` from `scripts/brand-assets.mjs`). Open Graph: `public/og.jpg` (1200 × 630, same script). Glyphs (`Glyph.astro`): rounded 3px strokes on a soft tile, with the mark's tick on the thing being picked (intake, portal, client).

## Direction
Three jobs, three chapter colors, one stack. Intake (Fillout) = ember, portal (Softr) = cobalt, team (Noloco) = pine. Chapter colors are section-scale surfaces (hub hero band, home chapter panels, the `/start/` StackMap bars, the diagram's focal column, the "choose" verdict column, the start plan column, compare plates, hub end band) and 8px square swatches that name a product. Text on a chapter color is always `--on-chapter` / `--on-chapter-muted`, never ink. Primary actions are ink (light theme) or paper (dark theme).

## Type
One family: Archivo Variable. Display `wdth` 104, tracking −0.035em. H1 weight 780, leading 0.98. H2 weight 740, leading 1.1, `--step-4` = `clamp(2rem, 1.5rem + 1.8vw, 3rem)` (48px max, after ClickUp). Other steps: `--step-3` ≤ 2.25rem, `--step-5` ≤ 4.5rem, `--step-6` ≤ 6rem (home hero, kept at ~79px at 1440 on purpose: see `BENCHMARK.md`). Program hero H1: `clamp(2.5rem, 1.6rem + 2.4vw, 3.75rem)`, leading 1.06, 14ch, 3 lines max. Article H2 leading 1.15. Body 17px, 1.6. UI labels 14–15px, weight 560–650. Tabular figures for prices and data. Prose lists keep `disc` / `decimal` markers (direct prose children only; component lists stay unmarked). Section heads: headline, then at most one line. No eyebrows or kickers.

## Shape, space, surfaces
Radii: `--r-btn` 10, `--r-frame` 12 (images), `--r-card` 16 (joined grids, diagram frame, covers), `--r-block` 20 (color blocks). `--section` 56–96px. Groups open with rules, not boxes: 2px ink rule on tables and fact lists, hairlines between rows. No card inside card, no mats, no side stripes. Gradient fields, grain and glow are allowed since 30 Sep 2026 (owner decision D1).

Media composition (v11): `.field` is a chapter color lit from `--field-at` (top right by default, the foot on phones; components with media on top pin it to the top) with `--field-lift` of the chapter color surviving at the light (80% on ember, 70% otherwise) and an SVG grain layer. `.field-wash` does the same on the light wash. `.plane` is a vendor screen lifted off a field (`--lift-field`); `.plane-angle` lays it back in 3D; `.float` overlaps a detail plane. `Scene.astro` composes field, main plane (or looped recording) and float; `Cover.astro` composes the card covers. Text on any field uses full `--on-chapter` for small text where the lit area would cut 90% below AA; check with `fieldcheck.mjs` on rendered pixels.

## Workflow and diagrams
- Home "Three jobs, three tools.": sticky rail + three lit chapter panels. Rail (v12, after Airtable's app-building rail): ruled rows with the chapter glyph, job, tool and number; the current row lifts onto a surface and its glyph takes color; a bar under each row fills with that chapter's share of the scroll. The current chapter's line swaps under the rows (instantly under reduced motion). Panels: title, meta, facts, actions under the facts, then a staged scene bleeding off the right and bottom edges. A screen scroller follows.
- `StackMap` (`/start/`): three adjoining lit chapter bars joined by chevron points, each with its job's real screen at the top, then badge, "From intake: …" handoff line, job / tool link / detail at the foot. The bypass is one sentence with a text link underneath.
- Hubs: `FlowStage` (v12, after Mintlify's line bundles): source tiles, a bundle of fine gradient lines running behind the product's real screen to a lock where it narrows to the lines that pass, then who sees the result; a light travels along the passing lines (motion only). One card per stop under it; phones read the cards joined by a chapter line, with the product screen in its card.
- Articles: `FitDiagram` rows (no crop, every width, max 44rem) with vertical arrows; `FlowDiagram` wraps it with numbered stops. `LayerDiagram` and `EntityDiagram` share its type.
- Details (`.details`, also plain `<details>` in prose): ruled rows with a round plus that fills ink and becomes a minus; open rows grow (`::details-content` + `interpolate-size`) and their text fades in; instant under reduced motion. On hubs the heading holds a sticky left column beside one list.

## Blog (v13, 27 Oct 2026)
Benchmarked in `BLOG-BENCHMARK.md` (daily.dev, Mintlify, Customer.io, Airtable; ClickUp, Amplemarket and Huly considered; Unicorn Studio has no public blog). ## Motion
GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis (`scripts/motion.ts`), only under `.motion-ok`. Moments: the home hero 3D stack (`HeroStack` + `stack3d.ts`, thinner bezels since v12) over a slowly drifting three-color light field, with the H1 words, then the lead and actions, revealed in beats; `data-reveal` on section heads, scenes and card rows; the home rail's segmented progress; the header's condensing bar and reading progress; the Tools panel's screen swap; the hub showcase's auto-advance with a fill line (stops once the reader picks); the flow stage's travelling light; accordion height and fade; `video[data-loop]` plays muted only while in view; `data-draw` diagram arrows and the footer mark draw once. Reduced motion: no Lenis, no WebGL, no drift, no auto-advance, no travelling light, no reveal or draw (everything shown complete), static poster, loop videos paused with native controls.

## Page anatomy
- Home: hero → broken-job list → chapter rail + lit panels → screen scroller → cost (joined grid) → comparisons (cover cards) → guides by stage → downloads → FAQ → footer stage with the closing statement.
- Hub: hero band (lit) with plan panel and the screen on the grid → verdict → (Softr) annotated screen → showcase → facts → how it fits (`FlowStage`) → plans → video → details → end band (lit), next steps, sources.
- Pricing: same hero without the screen → plan grid → worked examples → yearly vs monthly → what changes the bill → end.
- Indexes (`/guides/`, `/compare/`): title, lead and the mini stack; no breadcrumbs. Guide stages put the stage name in its own column beside the cards; odd counts open with one wide card.
- Blog index (`/blog/`): head → lead story + three latest → "Every post" with topic tabs → ruled rows → footer stage.
- Articles: an "All guides" / "All comparisons" / "All posts" back link (no breadcrumbs), title, lead, product links + reading time, a comparison cover beside the title on wide screens, rule-led short answer, TOC (ruled row on phones), prose. Guides carry no dates or disclosure blocks; blog posts show their date and a drawn banner cover, and end with sources and "More like this".
