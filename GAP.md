# GAP.md — what the v10 pass missed (29–30 Sep 2026)

The v10 benchmark pass matched **structure, type scale, and spacing** to the winners. The owner reviewed the result against `huly.io` and `airtable.com/platform/app-building` and judged it "a very cheap replica." This document records what is actually missing — the layer that carries "mind-blowing" — with evidence, honest verdicts, and what the next pass adopts.

Evidence: `.impeccable/live/bench-2026-09-29/` — `bench/` (benchmarks), `after/` (ours, current), plus owner-pasted renders of the live site.

## The honest verdict

Our pages hold roughly one media element every two or three sections. Benchmark pages hold one to three _composed media scenes per viewport_, and almost none of them are raw screenshots: they are staged, re-lit, angled, layered, or recorded. The structure pass was real but it bought the skeleton — the missing layer is media production, depth composition, motion density, texture, iconography and interactive chrome. That layer is most of what "premium" reads as on these sites.

## Media: what is actually available (the honest answer to "it's all online")

The benchmarks' media is custom-produced assets of _their_ products. We cannot download Huly's scenes or Airtable's mockups — their IP, and the wrong products anyway. For OUR products the equivalents are:

| Source                                                                                             | What it yields                                                                                                                                                         | Cost/risk                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Official vendor assets** — Softr/Noloco/Fillout marketing and press assets                       | Staged, art-directed product shots richer than the one raw capture per hero we use now                                                                                 | Free, fast; already allowed ("real vendor assets"). License-check each pull.       |
| **Own staged captures** — free-tier accounts in all three tools, seeded with realistic studio data | Consistent screenshots at fixed viewports/states/themes → composites; plus **screen recordings → muted loop cards** (the "video" benchmarks get from production crews) | Highest honesty + control; the real work of the pass (hours of staging, not code). |
| **Built vector illustration**                                                                      | Chapter glyphs, isometric slab motifs, diagram marks, cover system                                                                                                     | Real artwork we draw; no licensing risk.                                           |
| **Stock**                                                                                          | Abstract texture (grain/noise) only                                                                                                                                    | People photography rejected — generic stock humans cheapen a review site.          |
| **Not usable**                                                                                     | Benchmark artwork (IP + wrong product), other sites' screenshots (licensing gray), AI-generated "product shots" (= fake screenshots, still banned)                     | —                                                                                  |

## Stays banned regardless

Fake screenshots, fake testimonials, fake logos, benchmark IP (mockups, illustrations, copy), meaningless motion. The richness comes from _art direction and production of real material_, not invention.

## Suggested pass plan

- **P0 — decisions + asset production.** Resolve D1–D4; pull vendor assets; create the three free accounts; stage and capture screenshots + recordings; draw the SVG motif/glyph library.
- **P1 — composition system.** Media-field, angle, overlap and bleed tokens in `global.css`; the frame/figure primitives every later step uses.
- **P2 — hero + home.** Media scene per chapter panel; rail progress + crossfade; remove the invented arrows; hero reveal choreo.
- **P3 — StackMap + FitDiagram.** Media in bars and canvas cells (F2, F4).
- **P4 — pricing + guides covers.** Feature-row density, toggle, hover (F3); cover system + card grid (F6).
- **P5 — motion pass + finish.** D3 moments, reduced motion, then polish/harden/audit + build/verify as before.

Same rules as v10: one commit per section, 1440/390 (+ dark) screenshots against the benchmark crop, keep/revise-once/revert gate, real assets only.
