## What this is

A guide to a specific workflow: collecting a brief from a client, showing that client their records in a portal, and running the same records as team operations. Three products map to that workflow:

- **[Fillout](/fillout/)**: intake forms that write structured records.
- **[Softr](/softr/)**: a client portal on top of records you already keep.
- **[Noloco](/noloco/)**: a portal where staff and clients work in the same app.

The [blog](/blog/) covers the wider job around those tools: intake, portals, operations, AI builders, web basics and buying software.

The name is the job: a well-made pick, including the pick to buy nothing. The site was called _The Client Stack_ until October 2026. It is independent: it is not run by, funded by, or approved by Softr, Noloco, or Zite (Fillout’s parent company).

## How the writing works

Facts come from official vendor documentation and pricing pages, each linked to its source on the page. Diagrams are conceptual: they describe boundaries, not real accounts. Product images are screens the vendors publish themselves, plus one capture of a public Fillout template form; none of them shows a build made by this site. The site runs no hands-on benchmarks. Where a claim cannot be traced to a vendor page, or two vendor pages disagree, the page says so instead of paraphrasing a vendor’s marketing.

There are currently **no affiliate links** on this site. Product links are ordinary official URLs. If that changes, the [disclosure](/disclosure/) will say which links are affiliate links, and the same sourcing standard will still apply.
