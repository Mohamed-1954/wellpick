A client opens the portal and sees "In review". Your team moved the project to "Approved" an hour ago. Nobody did anything wrong. The portal just has not caught up.

How fast it catches up depends on one technical choice: how the portal gets data from where your records live.

## Three ways a portal reads your records

**Live sync.** The portal copies your records into its own database and keeps the copy updated as changes happen at the source. Changes arrive within moments, though not always instantly.

**Scheduled sync.** The portal copies your records on a timer. A change at the source waits for the next run. Fine for a weekly summary, wrong for a same-day approval.

**Direct read with a cache.** The portal reads the source when a page loads, and keeps recent results for a short while to stay fast. Freshness depends on how the cache decides that something changed.

## Which sources use which

Noloco's sync docs are specific. Airtable and SmartSuite sync live, and Stripe too, with limited live sync for HubSpot. Google Sheets, MySQL, PostgreSQL and Xano sync on a schedule. Edits made in Noloco write back to the source straight away. A change to the shape of your data, such as a new field, is a separate schema sync.

Softr reads your data source directly and keeps its own cache. Its data-source guide recommends Softr Databases for a new build and treats an existing Airtable base as worth keeping.

| Where your records live | Noloco | Softr |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Airtable | Live sync (Build plan) | Direct read with cache |
| Google Sheets | Scheduled sync | Direct read with cache |
| The tool's own tables | Native | Native (Softr Databases) |

## The fields that lag

Plain fields, such as a status or a date someone typed, usually travel well. Calculated fields are harder, because the source may not mark a record as changed when only a formula result changes.

Both tools have public reports of this:

- In December 2025, a Softr user found that Airtable formula and lookup changes did not reach a live Softr app, because Airtable did not mark those records as updated. Softr staff suggested a last-modified field as a workaround, and Softr later rolled out a new Airtable sync.
- Noloco forum users report lookup-of-lookup fields that fill in only after the record is edited in Noloco.

These are user reports, not measurements. They show where to look.

## A five-minute freshness test

1. Open the portal as a test client in a private window.
2. In your source, change a plain field on that client's record, such as the status. Note the time.
3. Refresh the portal every thirty seconds until the change appears. Note the time.
4. Repeat with a formula field and with a field that comes through a linked record.
5. Write the results down: "Status: under a minute. Totals: after the next edit."

That one line tells you what you can promise clients and what needs a workaround.

## Workarounds that hold up

- **Show the source's time.** Add a "last updated" field that clients can see, so a stale status is at least honest.
- **Avoid deep lookups on client pages.** Store the value the client needs directly on the record they read.
- **Pick the live route for anything time-sensitive.** If approvals must be same day, keep those records in a source your portal syncs live, or in the portal's own tables.
- **Say it in the invitation.** "Status updates appear within a few minutes" sets an expectation you can keep.
