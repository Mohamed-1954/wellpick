Every pricing page shows two numbers for the same plan. The smaller one is usually "per month, billed yearly". It is the yearly price divided by twelve, paid up front. The bigger one is what you pay if you can stop after a month.

The discount is real. So is the commitment. Here is how to choose.

## What the discount is worth

List prices as published on 27 October 2026, in USD:

| Plan | Billed monthly | Billed yearly, per month | Paid up front for a year | Saving over 12 months |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Softr Basic | $25 | $19 | $228 | $72 |
| Softr Pro | $119 | $99 | $1,188 | $240 |
| Noloco Build | $79 | $65 | $780 | $168 |
| Fillout Starter | $19 | $15 | $180 | $48 |
| Fillout Pro | $49 | $40 | $480 | $108 |

The saving is the monthly price times twelve, minus the yearly price. Taxes, discounts and add-ons such as extra users are not included.

## The case for monthly, first

A yearly plan is a bet that three things will not change for twelve months:

1. **The tool fits.** You will not find a dealbreaker in week six.
2. **The plan fits.** You will not need more users, more records or a feature on the next plan up.
3. **The business fits.** The client work it supports will still be there.

In the first months of a new setup, all three are uncertain. Paying a little more for two or three months buys you the right to change your mind. That is often worth more than the discount.

## When yearly makes sense straight away

- **You have already run the setup on a free plan** with test data and know the limits you need.
- **The tool replaces something you have used for years,** and the job is stable.
- **Cash flow is not tight,** and a single payment does not squeeze anything else.

## Watch the plan, not just the price

The bigger risk is often the plan, not the billing period. A few examples from our pricing data:

- **Softr Basic** caps you at 5 client users and sells no extra users. A sixth client contact means Pro. A yearly Basic plan does not protect you from that jump.
- **Noloco Free** stops workflows when 100 monthly runs are used. If automations matter, you may need Build sooner than you think.
- **Fillout** charges no seats, but Free covers 1,000 responses a month. Over the limit, forms keep collecting and you upgrade, buy more or wait for the next month.

Check what triggers an upgrade before you commit to a year on any plan.

## A simple rule

Start monthly. Put a reminder in your calendar for the end of month three. If the setup is in daily use and you have not hit a plan limit, switch to yearly then. If you have, you have learned something for the price of a few months' difference.
