This site covers Fillout and may earn a commission from it; it has no relationship with Tally. This comparison covers one job, getting a client's brief into Airtable, and works from each vendor's help center.

## Where Tally is enough

A typical first-contact brief (who you are, what you need, budget, timeline, a file or two) creates one record and never needs changing by the client. Tally covers that completely on its free plan, with no response cap, provided you stay within its fair-use policy. If you already have an Airtable automation that routes new records, Tally slots into it without new costs.

Tally also stores form data in the EU (it is a Belgian company). That can matter if your clients ask where their answers are held.

## Where Fillout earns its fee

People building client portals on Airtable keep asking Softr's community forum for the same three things ([updating a record from an embedded form](https://community.softr.io/t/9609), [passing the logged-in user into the form](https://community.softr.io/t/2614)). They want to pass the logged-in client into the form, update a record instead of creating a new one, and link the submission to the right company. These are the jobs Fillout documents and Tally's Airtable guide does not.

- **Corrections without duplicates.** Send each client the update link for their own record. Before you do, move internal fields to a separate table or turn on “Restrict prefilled data”, because the link otherwise exposes the whole record.
- **A form only signed-in clients can use.** Starter's login pages verify the respondent. Tally's password is shared by everyone who has it.
- **One brief, several records.** An onboarding form that creates a company, two contacts and a project, already linked.

If none of those apply, Fillout's extra cost buys you little for intake.

## The decision

- **Choose Tally** when each enquiry is a new record, files are under 10 MB, and nobody needs to sign in.
- **Choose Fillout** when clients must update their own records, sign in to the form, or create linked records in one submission.
- **Choose Fillout Business** only if Airtable-level duplicate prevention or files over 20 MB are a requirement.
- **Use neither** if your portal's own forms already do the job. Softr's standalone forms are free and unlimited, and Noloco views include forms that create records.

Fillout's owner, Zite, now also sells an app and portal builder. That does not change how Fillout forms work.
