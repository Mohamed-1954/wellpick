## Current status

No vendor program has approved this site, and no tracked link exists. If that changes, this page will say so first. It will name the vendor, the date and which links became affiliate links, before those links go live.

## How links behave now

- Links to Softr, Noloco, Fillout and their documentation are ordinary outbound URLs.
- Following one takes you to the vendor’s site, where its own terms and privacy practices apply.
- A link is a citation or a way to inspect the source. It is not a recommendation to buy without checking your requirements.

## What would change if a program approves the site

1. Some links to the approved vendor would carry a tracking identifier. This page would list which ones.
2. Each tracked link would be labeled for readers and marked as sponsored for search engines.
3. If you bought a paid plan through such a link, the vendor would pay this site a share of your subscription for a limited time. The table below shows the published share.
4. The sourcing rules would not change. Pages would still say when another tool fits better, or when you should buy nothing.

### Where the vendors’ own pages disagree

- **Softr:** the 25%, 12-month, 90-day and PartnerStack figures appear only on the affiliate page. The program terms (last updated September 2025) say commission is pre-agreed with Softr, lasts at most 12 months, is calculated on net revenue less discounts, and may have a minimum payout threshold.
- **Noloco:** the affiliate page says 20% for 12 months. The partner terms (last updated 22 May 2024) include a tier table where the starting Bronze tier earns 20% in year one and 10% in year two. The same terms also say “for the first 12 months” in another clause. Only an approved affiliate’s dashboard would settle which applies.

## What that means in dollars

Arithmetic on list prices, billed yearly, for one new customer in their first year. Softr calculates on net revenue less discounts, so real amounts can be lower.

- **Softr Basic** at $228/year × 25% = **$57**.
- **Softr Pro** at $1,188/year × 25% = **$297**.
- **Noloco Build** at $65 × 12 = $780/year × 20% = **$156**.
- **Fillout Starter** at $180/year × 30% = **$54**.

Commission would pull against some of this site's recommendations. From the 6th client login, [Softr vs Noloco](/compare/softr-vs-noloco/) recommends Noloco Build on price, yet Softr Pro would pay this site nearly twice as much. The pages follow what you pay, not what we would earn.

Fillout's program also pays on Zite plans, and Zite now sells an app and portal builder that competes with Softr and Noloco. This site does not cover Zite.

## Editorial separation

Coverage choices follow the workflow this site explains (intake, portal, team operations), not commission potential. Softr receives the deepest coverage as the portal most guides build on. That is not a claim that it is the best product, or that you need all three. The [methodology](/methodology/) states what the site does and does not claim about products.

## Before tracked links are added

Before any affiliate link goes live, the vendor must accept this site into its program, and the governing terms, approved destinations and required disclosures must be confirmed. This page describes current practice; it is not legal advice.
