One Softr feature has two different answers. The pricing page lists “Conditional form logic” under **Basic**. The Softr forms docs say the in-app Conditional Form Block is on the **Professional plan and higher**. If your portal needs a conditional form inside the app, ask Softr support which plan includes it before you buy Basic. Standalone Softr Forms are documented as free, with unlimited forms and submissions.

## Worked examples: arithmetic, not recommendations

**Noloco Build plus Fillout Starter.** Suppose you pay Noloco monthly and Fillout yearly. The monthly equivalent is $79 + $15 = **$94**. The first payment is different: $79 for the first portal month plus $180 for the form year = **$259**. Over 12 months, at unchanged rates, the two subscriptions total $79 × 12 + $180 = **$1,128**. Pay both monthly and the year costs ($79 + $19) × 12 = **$1,176**. Pay both yearly and it costs $65 × 12 + $180 = **$960**, all upfront.

**Softr Basic plus Fillout Starter.** Both billed yearly: $228 + $180 = **$408** upfront, or $34/month as an equivalent. Fillout's Softr embed method needs a paid Softr account, so Free does not work for this pairing. Basic caps you at 5 client users. A sixth client contact means Pro: $1,188 + $180 = **$1,368** a year, both billed yearly.

**Buy nothing.** If a shared folder and a weekly email already keep clients informed, the cost is **$0** plus the time you spend now. A portal has to beat that.

These totals leave out extra seats, usage overage, data-source plans, storage, setup time and upkeep. [Softr vs Noloco](/compare/softr-vs-noloco/) prices both at six team sizes.

## Put the required permissions above the price

Documented boundaries:

- **Softr:** global *view* restrictions on all plans, including Free. Global *create, edit and delete* restrictions on Business and above.
- **Noloco:** record permissions on all plans. Field-level permissions and unlimited custom roles on Build and above. Free has 2 roles.
- **Fillout:** Airtable duplicate prevention on Business and Enterprise only. It works with email and single-line fields.

These are not matching feature bundles. If a plan lacks a control you need, it is not an alternative at any price. Run the [two-client test](/guides/client-portal-launch/) to decide which controls you need.

## Count people the way each vendor counts them

Count people, not client companies. One company can have several contacts.

- **Noloco** counts a seat once a user logs in. An invited person who never signs in does not count. A team member shares an email domain with the app's builders; everyone else is a client. A deactivated user frees the seat from the next billing cycle.
- **Softr** splits app users into team and client users by email domain. Builders are a separate count: 1, 2 or 3 included on Basic, Pro and Business.
- **Fillout** does not charge per seat. The response limit and plan features set the price.

**Checkpoint:** write down your number of team users, client contacts and builders. Then find the first plan on each product that holds all three.

## Keep monthly equivalents separate from cash due

A monthly equivalent is the yearly price divided by 12. It is not the amount due at checkout. It does not mean you can cancel monthly. Track three numbers separately: the monthly recurring equivalent, the upfront cash, and your estimated internal upkeep. Label estimates as estimates. Keep tax and currency conversion explicit.

Both portal vendors offer a trial. Noloco documents a 14-day trial of Build with no credit card. Softr documents a 7-day trial of Business features, extended by 7 days if you add a card. Both list a 50% discount for non-profits. Price the plan you would keep after the trial, not the trial.

## Which setup to price first

- **Price Softr first** if your records already live in Airtable or another source, and your client list fits the user caps above.
- **Price Noloco Build first** if staff and clients work in the same records and you need field-level permissions or more than 2 roles.
- **Add Fillout** only when a native form misses a specific need, such as payments, page branching or large uploads.
- **Buy nothing yet** if you cannot name the control or the task a portal must deliver.
